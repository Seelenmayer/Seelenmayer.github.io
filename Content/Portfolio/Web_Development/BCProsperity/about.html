B.C. Prosperity Project

A Grassroots Civic Movement

The B.C. Prosperity Project exists to educate, unite, and empower British Columbians to take ownership of their future and rebuild confidence in democratic governance.

We are a grassroots, citizen-led civic movement — not a political party and not a lobby group. We believe real change starts with honest discussion, clear information, and shared conviction.

We do not want to leave Canada or British Columbia. But we recognize that Canada and B.C. have left many people behind economically, socially, and politically.

Our Mission Is To

Clarify, Educate, and Organize

1.

2.

3.

Purpose Statement

Educate, Inspire, Unite

Our purpose is to educate, inspire, and unite British Columbians, businesses, and organizations by highlighting the unique strengths and future potential of BC sovereignty.

We’re committed to fostering understanding, building connections, and addressing concerns through honest dialogue and meaningful engagement.

Who We Are

Citizen-Driven and Volunteer-Supported

The B.C. Prosperity Project is a citizen-driven movement focused on prosperity, accountability, and self-determination for British Columbians.

  • Publish educational resources, research, and policy discussions.
  • Provide fact-based discussion pieces on issues facing B.C.
  • Host town halls, presentations, and community outreach.
  • Build shared understanding of independence and constitutional reform options.
  • Mobilize grassroots support for democratic processes.

We do not receive government funding. We rely on volunteers, supporters, and collaborative partnerships.
